About

Welcome to Mysa.

your local hair & beauty spot

On Well Street in Hackney, Mysa is a busy, friendly studio where you can book everything from a quick trim to a full colour refresh. The team works with quality products and keeps prices accessible, so regular upkeep feels easy.

Whether you’re after a low-maintenance cut, bold colour change, or a beauty add-on, the vibe is relaxed and the service is straightforward. Great for students, locals and anyone who wants good hair without the fuss.
